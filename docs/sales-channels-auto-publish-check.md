# Do your sales channels already publish new products automatically?

This is a two-minute check in your Shopify admin. Nothing to install, nothing
to change — just look, and report back what you find.

## Why this matters

When you create a product **by hand** in the Shopify admin, it normally lands
on every sales channel you have — Online Store, Shop, and whichever others
you've connected — the moment you make it Active.

Products created by this tool are different: unless a channel has a setting
that takes new products automatically, they don't reach any channel on their
own. A newer version of this tool fixes that — every product it creates now
gets made available to all your channels as it's created, so flipping one to
Active is one click instead of six.

**But if your channels already take new products automatically, that fix does
nothing you didn't already have** — and it isn't worth carrying. That's the
only reason this check exists: to find out before, rather than after.

## What to do

1. Open your Shopify admin.
2. Go to **Settings → Apps and sales channels**, and look at the list of
   channels you have installed (probably something like Online Store, Shop,
   and a few others).
3. For **each channel** on that list, open it and look for a setting along
   the lines of *"Automatically publish new products"*, *"Auto-publish"*, or
   similar — the exact wording differs by channel.
4. Write down, for each channel, whether that setting is **on** or **off** —
   or whether you couldn't find any such setting at all.

You don't need to change anything. Just look, and note what each channel says.

## What to send back

A short list, one line per channel, like:

```
Online Store — on
Shop — on
Google & YouTube — off
TikTok — couldn't find a setting
```

That's it — nothing more detailed than that is needed.

## What happens with your answer

- **If every channel already says "on"**, the fix this tool now has isn't
  needed for your store, and should be turned off or removed rather than kept
  around unused.
- **If any channel says "off" or has no such setting**, the fix stays — it's
  doing something real for that channel.

Either way, nothing changes on your end until you're told otherwise. This is
just gathering the answer.
