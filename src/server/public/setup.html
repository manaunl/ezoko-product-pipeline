Setup

Connect this tool to your Shopify store and your Google spreadsheet. Everything you type here is saved on this computer only, in a file called .env.

1 Shopify

Shopify needs an app of its own before it will let this tool add products.

  1. Go to dev.shopify.com/dashboard and create an app.
  2. Set its access scopes to read_products, write_products, read_inventory, write_inventory, read_locations, read_publications, write_publications — then release that version. Skipping the release is the most common mistake.
  3. On the app's Home page, choose Install app and pick your store.
  4. Copy the Client ID and Client secret into the boxes below.
The app and the store must belong to the same Shopify organization. If your store isn't in the list when you try to install, it lives somewhere else and this won't work.
The .myshopify.com address, found in Shopify under Settings → Domains.

2 Google

This lets the tool read your spreadsheet and the photo folder, and write its results back.

  1. Go to console.cloud.google.com and create a project.
  2. Under APIs & Services → Library, turn on the Google Sheets API and the Google Drive API.
  3. Under OAuth consent screen, choose External, fill in the name and your email, and add your own Google address as a test user.
  4. Under Credentials, create an OAuth client ID of type Desktop app, and copy its ID and secret below.
Leave blank to keep the one already saved.

3 Sign in to Google

Save the details above first, then sign in. Google will warn that the app isn't verified — that's expected for a private tool. Choose Advanced, then Go to Ezoko.

This sign-in expires after 7 days and you'll need to do it again. That's a Google rule for tools that haven't been through their review process.
